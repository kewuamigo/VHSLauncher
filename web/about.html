VHS LAUNCHER

A home screen for Android TV where every app is a VHS cassette on a shelf. Pick one and it loads into an Orion GHV-1223 P, the Hungarian-market video recorder of the late 1980s, then the app starts.

This page is the browser preview. On a TV it is a native Kotlin app drawn with OpenGL ES; here the same scene runs on WebGL and apps open in a frame.

Download the Android TV APK, then install it with adb install vhs-launcher.apk.

CONTROLS

  • ← → or the mouse wheel: choose a tape
  • Enter or a click on the middle tape: load it
  • Esc or the back button in the corner: take it out
  • The red SETUP tape at the end: app order, sound, weather and more
